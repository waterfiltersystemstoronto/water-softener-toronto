## Why Hard Water Matters in Toronto

Hard water contains minerals such as **calcium and magnesium**, which can gradually accumulate inside plumbing, water heaters, dishwashers, washing machines, and other appliances. Over time, this buildup can affect efficiency and increase the need for cleaning and maintenance.

Toronto homes can vary significantly in age, plumbing configuration, household size, and water usage. Whether you live in a detached home in **North York**, a family property in **Scarborough**, a townhouse in **Etobicoke**, or another Toronto neighbourhood, the right system should be selected according to your home's specific water requirements.
